FILIPINO CULTURE AND TRADITIONS

Filipino culture is a vibrant, warm, and deeply relational blend of indigenous Austronesian roots, Spanish colonial history, American influences, and regional Asian trade. . Shaped across an archipelago of over 7,000 islands, it is defined by a strong sense of community, faith, and hospitality.

COMMON EXAMPLES OF FILIPINO CULTURE AND TRADITIONS

The Philippines is an archipelagic nation in Southeast Asia located in the western Pacific Ocean. It comprises 7,641 islands with a total land area of approximately 300,000 square kilometers (115,831 square miles). It is surrounded by bodies of water, including the Philippine Sea & Pacific Ocean found in the east, South China Sea found in the west, Celebes Sea & Sulu Sea found in the South, and lastly Bashi Channel found in the North.

It sits along the Pacific Ring of Fire, making it famous for active and uniquely shaped volcanoes, including Mayon Volcano found in Albay and Taal Volcano found in Batangas.
