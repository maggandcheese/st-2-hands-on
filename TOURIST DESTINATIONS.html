TOURIST DESTINATIONS

Boracay, Philippines

-> found in the province of Aklan, in the Western Visayas region, a tropical paradise famous for its 4-kilometer powdery white sand on White Beach, turquoise water, and glowing golden sunsets.

-> famous activities: island hopping, snorkeling, and water sports (parasailing, paddle boarding, jet skiing)

-> famous attractions: white beach, bulabog beach, and puka shell beach,


El Nido, Philippines

-> found in the province of Palawan, in the MIMAROPA region under the Luzon Island group, a tropical paradise in Palawan, Philippines, featuring towering limestone karst cliffs, hidden emerald lagoons, and crystal-clear turquoise waters.

-> famous activities: island hopping, cliff climb, and beach zipline

-> famous attractions: big lagoon, small lagoon, snake island, and hidden beach


Bohol, Philippines

-> an island province, located in the Central Visays Region, a tropical treasure in the Philippines blending surreal inland wonders with powder-white beaches. Here in Bohol, stands the iconic Chocolate Hills over 1,200 symmetrical, cone-shaped limestone mounds. In addition, it is a home to some of the planet's most endearing creatures, including the tiny, wide-eyed Philippine tarsier.

-> famous activities: sightseeing, island hopping, and zip lining

-> famous attractions: chocolate hills, bilar man-made forest, along beach, and philippine tarsier sanctuary


Baguio City, Philippines

-> located within the Province of Benguet, under the Cordillera Administrative Region (CAR) in Northern Luzon, Philippines, highlighting its cool mountain climate, pine-scented air, and rich Cordillera heritage as the Summer Capital of the Philippines.

-> famous activities: boating, biking, & horseback riding

-> famous attractions: burnham park, camp john hay, lion’s head, the mansion & write park, and baguio cathedral
